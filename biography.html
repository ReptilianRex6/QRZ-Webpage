About Me

Hi there! I'm Ethan, and I enjoy amateur radio, experimenting with different gear, and learning about Meshtastic and off-grid communications. I'm especially interested in HF, VHF/UHF, antennas, portable operation, repeaters, and digital communication projects.

If we've made contact, thanks for the QSO! I log using WRL, which automatically updates my QRZ logbook. I usually export to LoTW within 48 hours.
